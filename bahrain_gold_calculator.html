How to use it

Enter the gold rate and weight shown by the jewellery shop. Add the shop's making charge and choose whether it is charged per gram or as one fixed amount. Enter the VAT rate quoted by the shop. The calculator estimates the total.

Important: The default VAT is editable and is only an example. Confirm the applicable VAT and tax treatment with the Bahrain jeweller. This estimate excludes stones, discounts, other fees and any special tax treatment. The app does not fetch live gold rates.
